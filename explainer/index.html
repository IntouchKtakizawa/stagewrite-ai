FAQ

Before the first chord.

Is StageWrite AI only for guitarists?

No. The harmony, playback, metronome, transposition, saving, and export tools work for songwriters and producers generally. Guitar is the first instrument with dedicated visual diagrams.

Does it write a complete song?

It focuses on the harmonic foundation and the workflow around it. Melody, lyrics, arrangement, and production remain creative choices for the musician.

What makes it different from a chord dictionary?

A dictionary tells you which chords exist. StageWrite chooses progressions in context, explains the movement, and helps you turn the result into something playable.

Do I need an account?

No. The current app runs in the browser and stores projects locally. Microphone permission is requested only when you choose to use the tuner.

Can I move the result into a DAW?

Yes. Export MIDI for production, MusicXML for notation, or a chord chart for rehearsal and sharing.
